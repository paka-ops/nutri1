Country → languages → local foods → local recipes → local evidence → local recommendations → national intelligence.

This package is an advanced architecture/prototype foundation. Real production deployment still requires approved datasets, real authentication, cloud infrastructure, production AI models, device integrations, payment-provider credentials, security testing, legal/privacy review, scientific validation and government authorization.


## V50 institutional layer
- FAO → Food Systems Intelligence
- WFP → Food Security & Nutrition Intelligence

All six layers share the NUTRI.N°1 CORE: African Food Database, AI Engine, Food Scanner, Data Platform, GIS, Analytics, Early Warning, Interoperability, Privacy & Governance.


V51 adds a direct FAO Executive Home button and turns the Ministry of Health, Ministry of Agriculture, UNICEF, WHO, WFP, FAO, and Projects/Surveys/Nutrition Surveillance modules into an interactive live-application simulation with workflow feedback, AI/analytics processing states, validation steps, dynamic results and responsive feature cards. It retains the FAO Food Systems Intelligence Center and cross-institutional M&E/surveillance workspace covering baseline/midline/endline, impact evaluation, nutrition surveys, food-security surveys, dietary/anthropometric assessments and routine surveillance.
