# NUTRI.N°1 V20 — Condition-Specific Healthy Food Marketplace

Built on V19.

The Healthy Food & Nutrition Marketplace now has a dynamic patient-context layer.

Contexts:
- Diabetes
- Hypertension
- Kidney / renal nutrition
- Anemia
- Malnutrition
- Overweight / obesity
- Child nutrition
- Pregnancy / lactation

The patient country is selected and local foods are dynamically prioritized from a country-specific food catalog.

Flow:
Patient context → Country → Local food database → Nutrition match → Traceable products → Professional review → Order.

Important: this is a nutrition-support and marketplace demonstration. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace medical treatment. Renal diets, pediatric malnutrition, pregnancy and therapeutic nutrition require individualized professional/clinical review. Production should use validated country-specific composition data, national guidelines, contraindication logic, verified product claims and professional authorization.
