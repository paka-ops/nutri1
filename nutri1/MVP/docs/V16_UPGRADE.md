# NUTRI.N°1 V16 — Dynamic ECOWAS + AES Edition

## Requested upgrades implemented in the product prototype

### Language
The interface is now English-first.

### Country coverage
Country-aware catalog includes the requested ECOWAS + AES scope:
Benin, Burkina Faso, Cabo Verde, Côte d’Ivoire, The Gambia, Ghana, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, Liberia, Mali, Mauritania, Niger, Nigeria, Senegal, Sierra Leone, Togo, Chad.

### Connected devices
Architecture now exposes automatic-sync workflows for:
- Apple Health
- Android Health Connect
- Bluetooth LE devices
- glucose meters / CGM
- blood-pressure monitors
- smartwatches / fitness bands
- smart scales
- laboratory result imports

Real device integrations require OS permissions, device SDKs/protocol support and approved APIs.

### Ministry of Health Command Center
Dynamic dashboards for:
- children: stunting, wasting, underweight, overweight, obesity
- pregnant women
- lactating women
- diabetes
- hypertension
- cardiovascular disease
- cancers
- kidney disease
- other priority NCDs
- GIS and time trends

### Ministry of Agriculture Food Intelligence
Dynamic food-group dashboards:
- cereals
- tubers & roots
- fruits & vegetables
- legumes
- oilseeds
- animal-source foods
- production
- consumption
- needs
- food gaps
- GIS

### African Food Cloud
Dynamic ingestion:
- manual entry
- photo / camera scan
- barcode / product label
- OCR
- laboratory result capture
- API/import
- ontology matching
- composition lookup
- provenance
- validation

## Data safety
The prototype visualizes illustrative chart structures. It must not be presented as real national prevalence data until validated national datasets are connected.
