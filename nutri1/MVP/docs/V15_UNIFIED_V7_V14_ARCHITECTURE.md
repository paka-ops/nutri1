V15 is the single dynamic product that combines the capabilities developed across V7, V8, V9, V10, V11, V12, V13 and V14.

- Country and language selection
- Anthropometric data
- Automatic BMI and body ratios
- Clinical data

- Food journal
- AI Food Scanner
- AI Meal/Recipe Scanner
- Personalized nutrition plan
- Malnutrition prevention
- NCD prevention
- Dashboard and time-series trends
- Nutritionist chat
- Appointment booking
- Payments
