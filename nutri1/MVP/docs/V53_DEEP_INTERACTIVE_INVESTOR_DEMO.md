# NUTRI.N°1 V53 — Deep Interactive Investor Demonstration

V53 extends V52 with a front-end operational simulation layer for the institutional intelligence centers:

- Ministry of Health
- Ministry of Agriculture
- UNICEF
- WHO
- WFP
- FAO
- Projects, Surveys & Nutrition Surveillance

## Demonstration experience

Any meaningful card, KPI, indicator, alert, age group, risk row, table row, workflow item or institutional control can open a simulated operational workflow.

The workflow demonstrates:

1. institutional workspace authentication;
2. source connector orchestration;
3. country / geography / year context resolution;
4. schema, completeness and provenance validation;
5. NUTRI.N°1 AI signal fusion;
6. anomaly and risk scoring;
7. GIS and early-warning refresh;
8. decision and intervention generation;
9. M&E traceability and audit trail.

The simulation includes interactive tabs for Data & Provenance, GIS, Early Warning, Decision Center and Audit Trail, plus shock scenarios, intervention creation, team assignment, impact tracking, source synchronization, quality gates and executive brief generation.

All displayed values are demonstration values. No external institutional system is modified by this front-end simulation.
