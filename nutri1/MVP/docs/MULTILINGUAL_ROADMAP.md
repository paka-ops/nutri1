# Multilingual roadmap

## Tier 1
Official language interface per country.

## Tier 2
Major national/local languages.

## Tier 3
Food-name ontology, synonyms and voice.

## Tier 4
Full conversational nutrition AI in selected local languages.

## Validation
Translation must be reviewed by:
- native speakers
- nutrition professionals
- country data stewards
- community representatives where appropriate.

The system must support code-switching, local food names and culturally specific expressions.
