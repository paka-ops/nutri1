# NUTRI.N°1 V51 — Interactive Institutional Simulation

V51 adds an Executive Home FAO shortcut and a reusable interaction layer across the institutional intelligence modules.

## Interactive modules
- Ministry of Health
- Ministry of Agriculture
- UNICEF
- WHO
- WFP
- FAO
- Projects, Surveys & Nutrition Surveillance

## Simulation behavior
Feature cards, KPI tiles, risk rows, alert rows, age cards and condition cards can be activated. The interface simulates a production workflow: institutional data connection, quality/provenance validation, NUTRI.N°1 AI/analytics processing, dashboard/GIS/early-warning refresh, then a contextual result.

Country, geography/level and year changes also trigger visible refresh feedback.

## Important
This is a front-end simulation, not a claim that live institutional APIs or official national databases are already connected. Production deployment requires authorized datasets, authentication, security/privacy controls, institutional agreements, validated scientific indicators and tested integrations.
