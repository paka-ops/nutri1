# NUTRI.N°1 V19 — Healthy Food & Nutrition Marketplace

Built on V18.

Adds a traceable African healthy-food marketplace connected to nutrition goals and professional follow-up.

Modules:
- Biofortified staples
- Nutrient-rich flours
- Fruits & vegetables
- Legumes & seeds
- Healthy oils
- Functional foods
- Nutrition supplements
- Country/category/goal filtering
- Patient-oriented recommendations
- Product traceability
- Cart
- Country-specific checkout handoff

Supplements are presented as professionally governed products; real production requires regulatory review, contraindication checks, verified claims, supplier onboarding and national compliance.
