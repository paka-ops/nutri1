# Data Sources & Provenance

Potential source classes:
- FAO/INFOODS
- national food composition tables
- national statistics
- agriculture ministries
- health ministries
- household consumption surveys
- research institutions
- laboratory analyses
- peer-reviewed studies
- validated recipe surveys
- user-generated data, with explicit labeling and quality controls

No source should be silently transformed into a "national truth". Every value keeps provenance, methodology, version and quality status.
