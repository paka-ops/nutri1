# NUTRI.N°1 V50.1 — FAO + Monitoring, Evaluation & Nutrition Surveillance

## FAO layer
The FAO Food Systems Intelligence Center connects production forecasting, post-harvest losses, markets and prices, healthy-food availability, nutrition-sensitive agriculture, food safety, traceability, processing/value addition and climate-food early warning.

## Cross-institutional M&E and surveillance layer
A shared institutional workspace supports programme baselines, indicator frameworks, routine monitoring, midline/endline, impact evaluation, nutrition surveys, food-security surveys, dietary assessments, anthropometry, maternal/child nutrition, NCD risk-factor surveys, market/food-price surveillance and automated evidence reports.

## Governance
All production deployments require validated authorized datasets, consent where applicable, data minimization, role-based access, provenance, quality checks, scientific validation and human review. Demonstration values in the prototype are illustrative and must not be presented as official national statistics.
