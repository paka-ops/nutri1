# Executive Positioning

## NUTRI.N°1
### Africa's Food & Nutrition Intelligence Infrastructure

NUTRI.N°1 connects three previously separated layers:

1. PERSON — what I eat, my body, my metabolic context and my prevention.
2. FOOD SYSTEM — what a country produces, consumes and needs.
3. PUBLIC HEALTH — where nutrition problems and opportunities exist.

The strategic moat is the country-aware African food intelligence layer:
food ontology + composition + recipes + consumption + production + evidence + AI + professionals.

## Value
For individuals: actionable culturally relevant prevention.
For governments: population intelligence for health and agriculture.
For institutions: trusted, versioned, governed African nutrition data.
