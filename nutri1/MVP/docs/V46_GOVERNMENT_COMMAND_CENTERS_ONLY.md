# NUTRI.N°1 V46 — Government Command Centers Only

Only the Ministry of Health Command Center and Ministry of Agriculture Food Intelligence pages are enhanced. Other validated modules are not intentionally changed.

Ministry of Health:
- Live intelligence status
- KPI cards
- Population segment filter by age
- Children — double burden chart
- Pregnant & lactating women chart
- NCD trends chart
- Refresh indicator interaction

Ministry of Agriculture:
- Live intelligence status
- KPI cards
- Food-group filter
- Production by food group chart
- Consumption vs needs chart
- Food supply trend chart
- Refresh indicator interaction

Charts use Chart.js when available. Existing page structure is preserved as much as possible; the new government layer is additive.
