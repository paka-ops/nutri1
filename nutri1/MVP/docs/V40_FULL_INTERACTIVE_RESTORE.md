V40 keeps the V20/V38 visual baseline and V17 payment presentation, while restoring interactive behavior requested for the main modules.

- Citizen Dashboard 360°: Anthropometry Trends, Nutrition-linked Clinical Parameters, Metabolic & Clinical Trends, Personalized Recommendations.
- Connected Devices: automatic synchronization progress animation.
- AI Food & Meal Scanner: scan controls produce a dynamic result panel.
- AI Nutrition & Prevention: Generate Personalized Plan produces a 7-day menu.
- Nutritionist Network: chat interaction is restored where the existing chat controls are present.
- Ministry of Health Command Center: Children Double Burden, NCDs Trends, Pregnant & Lactating Women charts.
- Ministry of Agriculture Food Intelligence: Production by Food Group, Consumption vs Needs charts.
- Research & Evidence Graph: Evidence Growth, Food Composition Growth, Recipe Validation, Evidence Intensity Heatmap chart layer.

Preserved:
- V20 dynamic visual baseline
- Country/language localization package
- WHO and UNICEF buttons/pages
- V17-style country-specific Payments
- Existing page structure and navigation

Chart.js is used when available; SVG fallback is provided for chart hosts where Chart.js is unavailable.
