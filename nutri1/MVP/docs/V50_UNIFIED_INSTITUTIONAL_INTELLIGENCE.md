# NUTRI.N°1 V50 — Unified Institutional Intelligence Architecture

## Strategic positioning
NUTRI.N°1 is an African Food, Nutrition & Health Intelligence Infrastructure. A single technology core serves citizens, professionals, governments, research institutions and development partners.

## NUTRI.N°1 CORE
1. African Food Database
2. AI Engine
3. Food Scanner
4. Data Platform
5. GIS
6. Analytics
7. Early Warning
8. Interoperability
9. Privacy & Governance

## Institutional layers

### FAO — Food Systems Intelligence
Production, post-harvest losses, food availability, markets, prices, processing, food safety, biofortification, climate and nutrition-sensitive agriculture.

### FAO — Food Systems Intelligence
Production forecasting, post-harvest losses, food availability, markets, prices, processing, food safety, traceability, biofortification, climate and nutrition-sensitive agriculture.

### WFP — Food Security Intelligence
Household food security, food consumption, dietary diversity, healthy-diet affordability, food prices, vulnerability, school feeding, local procurement, climate shocks, early warning and intervention impact.

### UNICEF — Child Nutrition Intelligence
Child growth, infant and young child feeding, maternal nutrition, anaemia, school nutrition, child nutrition hotspots and early nutrition signals.

### WHO — NCD Nutrition Intelligence
Dietary risk, diabetes, hypertension, cardiovascular disease, obesity, sodium, sugar, lifestyle, NCD risk factors, prevention and impact monitoring.

### Ministry of Agriculture — National Agrifood Intelligence
Farmer registry, production forecasting, climate and crop alerts, markets, stocks, post-harvest losses, processing, value addition, biofortification, food safety and national agrifood command center.

### Ministry of Health — National Nutrition & Health Intelligence
Nutrition surveillance, diabetes, hypertension, cardiovascular risk, obesity, screening campaigns, professional follow-up, NCD early warning, population dashboards and prevention impact.

## Shared data architecture
Field data → validation → provenance → ontology/entity resolution → aggregation → indicator engine → AI/analytics → GIS → dashboard → human decision → intervention → impact measurement.

## Data governance
Individual health and child data are consent-based, minimized, access-controlled and protected. Institutional dashboards should prioritize validated, aggregated indicators. Sensitive decisions require qualified human review.

## Open / proprietary strategy
Open-source selected non-core components and interoperability layers where appropriate. Protect proprietary datasets, validated AI models, commercially sensitive algorithms and intellectual property that create competitive advantage, subject to the requirements of each funding or implementation partner.

## Production readiness
The prototype remains a demonstration foundation. Production deployment requires validated national datasets, real authentication, cloud infrastructure, security testing, legal/privacy review, scientific validation, approved clinical/public-health protocols, institutional agreements and interoperability testing.

### Cross-institutional Monitoring, Evaluation & Surveillance
Programme M&E, baseline/midline/endline, impact evaluation, nutrition and food-security surveys, dietary and anthropometric assessments, NCD risk-factor surveys, routine surveillance, data-quality control, GIS coverage and automated evidence reporting.
