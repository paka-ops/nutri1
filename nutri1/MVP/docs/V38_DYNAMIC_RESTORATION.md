# NUTRI.N°1 V38 — V20 Dynamic Experience Restored

V38 preserves the V37 payment module and restores the dynamic V20-style experience across:
- Executive Home
- Citizen Dashboard 360°
- Connected Devices
- AI Food & Meal Scanner
- AI Nutrition & Prevention
- Nutritionist Network
- Healthy Food Marketplace
- African Food Cloud
- Ministry of Health Command Center
- Ministry of Agriculture Food Intelligence
- Research & Evidence Graph
- UNICEF Nutrition Intelligence Center
- WHO Nutrition & NCD Intelligence Center

Each detected page receives live-style KPI cards, an interactive period selector, refresh interaction and at least one chart. A lightweight SVG fallback keeps charts visible when Chart.js is unavailable.

V37 country/language/payment behavior is preserved. No replacement payment UI is introduced.
