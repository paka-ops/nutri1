# NUTRI.N°1 V57 — Exact Interactive Institutional Modules

V57 preserves V56 and adds feature-specific front-end workspaces. Clickable institutional modules now open a contextual simulation matching the exact feature label, with custom KPIs, trend chart, indicator profile, operational records, decision workflow, actions and contextual records.

Covered examples include Diabetes Prevention, Sugar Intelligence, Obesity Observatory, NCD Early Warning, Hypertension & CVD, Farmer Registry, Climate Early Warning, Post-Harvest Loss Observatory, Food Price Intelligence, IYCF, Maternal Nutrition, Anaemia Intelligence, WFP food-security modules, FAO agrifood modules, and M&E/Survey/Surveillance modules.

Demo values are illustrative front-end values and must not be presented as official statistics.
