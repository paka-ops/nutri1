# NUTRI.N°1 V58 — Fully bilingual MVP (English + French)

V58 preserves every V57 feature and makes the complete MVP available in
English. The six institutional dashboards (WHO, WFP, FAO, UNICEF, Ministry of
Health, Ministry of Agriculture), their Canvas charts, tooltips, CSV exports
and PDF reports now render in English whenever the platform language is
English, while the French experience remains exactly as before.

Selection: language selector in the top bar (English / Français / Español +
local languages). Switching is instant and reversible; nothing is destructive.

Also repaired in V58 (pre-existing breaks): the language selector crash when
picking "English" by name, the dead V48 country/language engine, the missing
citizen/profile/scanner/AI-plan action handlers, the boot-chain break in the
marketplace script, and the V34/V44/V29 scripting errors.

See `docs/V58_BILINGUAL_EN_DASHBOARDS.md` for the full technical report.
