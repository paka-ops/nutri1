# Security & Privacy Baseline

- default-deny
- tenant isolation
- RBAC
- consent management
- encryption in transit and at rest
- secrets management
- audit logging
- backup/disaster recovery
- incident response
- vulnerability management
- penetration testing
- data minimization
- aggregation/suppression for government dashboards
- no government access to identifiable citizen health records by default
- research access governed and scoped

Country-specific legal requirements must be assessed before production.
