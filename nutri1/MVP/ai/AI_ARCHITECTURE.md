# NUTRI.N°1 African Nutrition AI

## AI modules

### 1. Food Resolver
Image/text/voice → candidate food → country-aware ontology → canonical food.

### 2. Food Scanner
Image → food identification → estimated portion → nutrient profile.

### 3. Meal/Recipe Scanner
Image → dish recognition → recipe candidates → ingredient decomposition → estimated portion → nutrient profile.

### 4. Nutrition Coach
Food journal + profile + goals + country + evidence → personalized recommendations.

### 5. Prevention Engine
Anthropometry + diet + metabolic data + activity + clinical context + evidence → risk signals and prevention guidance.

### 6. Population Intelligence
Aggregated data → indicator trends → anomalies → GIS insights.

## Safety
AI must show uncertainty/confidence where relevant, preserve model version and evidence references, and avoid presenting a risk signal as a diagnosis.

## Human-in-the-loop
Nutritionists and authorized professionals can review recommendations and ambiguous scans.
