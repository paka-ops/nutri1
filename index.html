Africa's Food & Nutrition Intelligence Infrastructure

We are building Africa's nutrition infrastructure.

NUTRI.N°1 is a sustainable agro-processing and nutrition intelligence platform, starting in West Africa and scaling across the continent. We are building the digital infrastructure that connects food, agriculture, nutrition, health, climate, markets, and people — transforming data and food science into actionable intelligence, personalized nutrition, smarter production, and better food systems. We start with digital nutrition intelligence. We then connect intelligence, demand, food science, and sustainable agro-processing to build a competitive, nutrition-driven food ecosystem for Africa.

The ambition

Become Africa's leading nutrition company.

Digital infrastructure is the first layer. Sustainable agro-processing is the second. Together they create a closed loop between what Africa produces, what Africa eats, what Africa needs and what should be recommended.

Digital loop

Profile → food → AI → recommendation → prevention → professional support → follow-up.

Food-system loop

Nutrition needs → demand → production → transformation → marketplace → consumption → new intelligence.
